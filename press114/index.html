ONE SHOT, MANY POSSIBILITIES

Let the drops
carry the shot.

Drag to draw your bow and release to shoot. A struck raindrop splits into smaller droplets that can keep the chain going. Special rain changes how you approach each encounter.

Learn to split an arrow in flight, use rebounds, and choose up to three arrow effects for the journey ahead.

BEASTS OF ANCIENT MYTH

Read the rain.
Find a way through.

Beast encounters bring different patterns to learn. Against Dijiang, small drops must first pass through a vortex to break its armor.

Combat is only one part of the crossing. Between encounters, warm a cup of tea, listen to a stranger, and discover the keepsakes they leave behind.

BEFORE XIANG LIU

Know the crystals.

Upcoming update: The clearer briefing shown here is part of Rain Hunt 1.1, planned for Dec 7, 2026 (subject to review). Version 1.0 is available now; the crystal rules below are unchanged.

01

Twelve crystals. Three hits each.

Only droplets split from ordinary rain can break crystals.

02

A five-second window.

Up to four crystals that are not ever-lit light at random for five seconds. Hits do not add time. When the light fades, unfinished hits reset.

03

Know what you keep.

Keepsakes upgraded by defeating their beasts make matching crystals ever-lit. Only shattered, ever-lit crystals stay broken on your next journey. Temporary breaks and partial hits do not carry over.

04

One attempt this journey.

After three arrows, choose whether to spend your final arrow for one more shot. Return Lamp Tokens cannot be used here.
