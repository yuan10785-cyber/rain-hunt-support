Privacy policy

Developer: Yuan Li. This policy describes the Rain Hunt iPhone app and this support website. Privacy enquiries: airwalk3@hotmail.com.

Your journey stays on your device

The game stores journey progress, language preferences, and settings on your device. It does not require a game account. The current app has no developer-operated cloud-save service, advertising SDK, or third-party gameplay analytics SDK identified in the reviewed application code.

If you choose to export a save or share a screenshot, the data goes to the destination you choose. Starting a new journey replaces progress according to the game's confirmation prompt. Removing the app may remove local progress.

Purchases through Apple

Apple processes the optional full-game purchase. The app uses StoreKit to verify purchase entitlement and restore access. We do not receive your payment-card details. Apple handles its own account, payment, store, and diagnostic information under its privacy policies. Purchase entitlement and journey progress are separate.

When you contact us

If you email support, we receive your email address and the information you choose to send, including attachments. We use that information to respond to your request and investigate the issue. Microsoft Outlook/Hotmail processes messages to deliver this service. Please send only information needed for support, and never send passwords, security codes, or payment-card details.

We keep support correspondence for no longer than 12 months after the issue is resolved, then delete it, unless a longer period is required by law. Contact the same address to request access to, correction of, or deletion of information you sent us. Any legally required retention will be explained where applicable.

This website

Our website code does not add analytics, advertising scripts, tracking cookies, or embedded third-party media. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical request information, such as IP addresses, browser information and request times, to deliver and protect the website. Its processing and retention are governed by its applicable policies, not the 12-month support-email period above. See GitHub's privacy statement. Email links open your email application. External sites have their own policies.

Your choices

Sending support information is optional. We do not sell your support correspondence or use it for targeted advertising. Service providers may process information outside your country. You may contact us about your privacy rights under applicable law. Please do not submit children's personal information in a support request.

Changes

If our data practices change, we will update this policy and its effective date.
