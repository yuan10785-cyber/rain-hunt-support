Before you write

Please include your game version, iPhone or Mac model, operating system version, language, and the steps that led to the problem. A screenshot is helpful. Do not send passwords, verification codes, payment-card details, or other sensitive information.

What is included for free?

The first 10 stages are free. A single non-consumable in-app purchase unlocks the rest of the main journey. There is no subscription.

How do I restore my purchase?

Use the Apple Account that made the purchase. Open Settings in the game, go to the full-game purchase page, and choose Restore Purchases. Make sure you have an internet connection. Restoring a purchase restores access to the full game; it does not restore a lost journey save.

My purchase has not unlocked the game.

If Apple is still awaiting purchase approval, access will unlock after approval. Otherwise try Restore Purchases. If the problem persists, contact us with the on-screen message. Do not buy the game again to troubleshoot.

Will starting again remove my purchase?

No. Purchase access is separate from journey progress. Starting a new journey can replace your progress. Do not delete the app or reset your journey as a troubleshooting step without backing up your progress first.

How do I request a refund?

Apple handles App Store payment and refund requests. Visit Apple's Report a Problem service. Refund eligibility is determined by Apple.

How do I change the game window or return from a desktop companion on Mac?

Rain Hunt opens in a resizable portrait window that fits your display. Drag a window edge to adjust its size. Full-screen mode is currently unavailable. If you summon a desktop companion, the game window minimizes. Right-click the companion and choose Return to Rain Hunt to bring the game back.

How do I change the language?

Open Settings and select a language. The game includes English, Simplified Chinese, Japanese, and Korean.
